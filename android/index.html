Help bring Publit to Android

Publit is my app for buses, trams, metro and trains across Europe. It's already on the iPhone. Before Google lets it into the Play Store, 12 people need to keep it on their Android phone for 14 days.

The test opens in the next few days. Bookmark this page and come back, the buttons will work then.

What you need

  • An Android phone with Android 8 or newer
  • The Google account (Gmail address) that's signed in to the Play Store on that phone
  • Two minutes
  1. 1
    Join the tester group

    Open the group on your phone and tap "Join group". Use the same Google account as in the Play Store. No posts, no emails to answer.

    Join the group
  2. 2
    Become a tester

    On the Google Play page that opens, tap "Become a tester". It can take a few minutes until Google knows you're in the group, so try again later if it doesn't work right away.

    Become a tester
  3. 3
    Install Publit

    Tap "Download it on Google Play" on the same page, or search for Publit in the Play Store. Install it like any other app.

  4. 4
    Keep it for 14 days

    Just leave it installed and don't leave the test. Opening it now and then helps. Telling me what breaks helps even more.

Questions

I have an iPhone.

Then you don't need the test: Publit is already in the App Store.

Does it cost anything?

No. The test and the app are free.

I have a Wear OS watch.

Optional. Once the watch app is part of the test, the Play Store on your watch offers it too. It shows your trip while you're on the way.

What happens after 14 days?

You can keep the app. When Publit is public on Google Play, you get updates like with any other app.
